About Us

We don't follow the season. We set it.

GEN_Z started in a garage with three sewing machines and a refusal to make clothes that looked like everyone else's. Today we're a collective of designers, welders, and archivists building pieces that read like a manifesto.

Every drop is engineered small on purpose — limited runs, obsessive construction, zero filler. We build for the people who show up first and leave a mark.
